# Compliance flags (flag, don't advise)

Raise the relevant points in every agent pack. **This is not legal advice.**
The pack must say a lawyer in the target market confirms them before scaling.
Laws change; check the current rules for the market. If the market isn't
listed here, say the rules differ and must be checked.

## Everywhere
- **Never claim to be human.** Answer honestly if asked whether it's AI.
  Proactive disclosure is the safe default for outreach, callbacks and
  customer calls.
- **Identify early:** agent name, company, purpose of the call.
- **Honour opt-outs immediately** and record them so the number is never
  called again.
- **Don't invent** customers, results, prices or urgency (misleading conduct).
- **Recording consent:** if calls are recorded, say so near the start unless
  legal sign-off says otherwise.
- **Collect only what the use case needs**, and don't read out personal or
  account details before confirming identity.
- **Mystery shops that feed a sales follow-up** can turn a research call into
  telemarketing and raise misleading-conduct risk. Flag it.

## United States
- **TCPA:** the FCC has ruled that AI-generated voices count as "artificial
  or prerecorded voice", so calls to consumers' phones generally need prior
  express consent (prior express *written* consent for telemarketing).
  Business-to-business landlines differ; mobiles are treated strictly.
- **National Do Not Call Registry** and company-specific do-not-call lists for
  telemarketing to consumers. Some states keep their own lists.
- **Calling hours:** 8am-9pm callee's local time federally; several states are
  stricter.
- **State "mini-TCPA" laws** (e.g. Florida, Oklahoma) and state AI-disclosure
  bills add rules.
- **Call recording:** some states require all-party consent (e.g. California,
  Florida, Washington).

## United Kingdom
- **PECR:** live marketing calls must not go to numbers on the TPS/CTPS unless
  the person consented; automated calls generally need specific prior
  consent. Display a callable number.
- **UK GDPR** applies to recordings and transcripts.
- Ofcom persistent-misuse rules cover abandoned and silent calls.

## European Union
- **GDPR:** lawful basis for processing call data, transparency, data
  minimisation, retention limits.
- **EU AI Act:** people must be told they're interacting with an AI system
  unless it's obvious.
- National rules on unsolicited calls vary (opt-in vs opt-out registers by
  country).

## Australia
- **Do Not Call Register Act 2006 and the Telemarketing and Research Calls
  Industry Standard** apply to calls promoting goods or services, whether made
  by a person, a recording or AI.
  - Calling hours: weekdays 9am-8pm, Saturday 9am-5pm (callee's local time).
    No Sundays or national public holidays.
  - Identify caller name, company and purpose early; show a callable number;
    end the call when asked.
  - Personal numbers must be washed against the register for telemarketing.
- **Australian Consumer Law:** misleading or deceptive conduct.
- **Call recording:** consent rules differ by state; most require all parties
  to know.
- **Spam Act 2003** covers follow-up SMS and email: consent, sender
  identification, working unsubscribe.
- **Privacy Act:** collect only what's needed; say how details will be used if
  asked.
